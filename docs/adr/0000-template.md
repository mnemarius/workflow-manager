# NNNN — Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-XXXX](xxxx-....md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play — the problem, the constraints, the requirements. Link the relevant
section of [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) or [BUSINESS.md](../BUSINESS.md).

## Decision

The change we are making, stated in active voice: "We will …".

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include trade-offs accepted and options rejected.

---

## Process

ADRs are numbered sequentially and **forward-only** — never edit an accepted ADR's decision;
supersede it with a new one and update the old one's status. One ADR per major architectural
decision (see the "Key design decisions" in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md)).
